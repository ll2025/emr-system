# Initial Research on EHR's

## Basic Terminology 
EHR = electronic health record that can be shared among many systems, practices, facilities, and more.

EMR = electronic medical record that stays within one system or practice location

## Necessary Components
Patients':
-   demographics: name, date of birth, sex  
-   contact information: address, phone number, emergency contact information
-   medical history
-   allergies
-   diagnoses
-   medications: current and past prescriptions
-   immunizations
-   surgeries/procedures
-   purchases of eyewear, prescriptions, and/or other products
-   past examinations
-   future scheduled examinations

## Example EHR Platforms
- RevolutionEHR
- Eyefinity Encompass
- MaximEyes
- and more ...

## Set-Backs
- Many platforms are not able to function without a connection to the Internet. So, at off-grid or rural locations, these platforms are unusable. 
- Need to be able to store all the records in a location where they can be accessed anywhere in the world without causing additional charges for storage and management.
- Commercial EHR's require a subscription to use the services. Not feasible for non-profits or practices with limited resources.
